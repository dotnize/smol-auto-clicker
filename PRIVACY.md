# Privacy policy for Smol Auto Clicker

Last updated: October 22, 2026

The app runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, store, or transmit
personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking, and
makes no network requests.

The app uses Windows APIs to simulate mouse clicks and button holds and to
register the F6 and F7 shortcuts. It does not record your keyboard or mouse
activity or read the contents of other applications. Settings are kept in memory
only and are discarded when the app closes.

Microsoft Store and Windows may process information independently under
[Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).
Visiting this policy on GitHub is subject to
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

This policy will be updated if the app's privacy practices change.

For support or privacy questions, email [nat@nize.ph](mailto:nat@nize.ph).
